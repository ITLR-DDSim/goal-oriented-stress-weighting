# External data sources

The references below identify the external data sources used in the study. Datasets are not distributed in this repository. Publication links provide the source papers; dataset access is listed separately where available.

## Periodic-hill DNS

Xiao, H., Wu, J.-L., Laizet, S., and Duan, L. (2020). **Flows over periodic hills of parameterized geometries: A dataset for data-driven turbulence modeling from direct simulations.** *Computers & Fluids*, **200**, 104431.

- [Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compfluid.2020.104431)
- [Dataset and access instructions — Turbulence Modeling Resource](https://tmbwg.github.io/turbmodels/Other_DNS_Data/parameterized_periodic_hills.html)

## Square-duct DNS

Pinelli, A., Uhlmann, M., Sekimoto, A., and Kawahara, G. (2010). **Reynolds number dependence of mean flow structure in square duct turbulence.** *Journal of Fluid Mechanics*, **644**, 107–122.

- [Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112009992242)
- [Institutional publication record](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/15264/)

## Film-cooling experiment (BR = 1.0)

Schroeder, R. P., and Thole, K. A. (2014). **Adiabatic Effectiveness Measurements for a Baseline Shaped Film Cooling Hole.** *Proceedings of ASME Turbo Expo 2014: Turbine Technical Conference and Exposition*, Vol. 5B, paper GT2014-25992.

- [Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.1115/GT2014-25992)
- [Author-hosted paper (PDF)](https://cpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.psu.edu/dist/c/92759/files/2018/08/2014-Schroeder-IGTACE-1-1zop7wx.pdf)

## 7-7-7 jet-in-crossflow LES

The LES data were provided by Prof. Solkeun Jee’s group at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST). Please contact the group regarding access.

The LES fields are not included in the planned release. Jet-in-crossflow case files remain excluded pending coordination with the data providers.

## Use and attribution

Please cite the original publications when using their data and follow the data providers’ access and reuse conditions. Links to papers do not imply permission to redistribute the associated datasets.
